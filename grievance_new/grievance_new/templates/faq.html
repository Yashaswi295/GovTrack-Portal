Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about submitting and tracking grievances.

GovTrack provides a digital platform where citizens can report civic issues and track public project progress using AI and geospatial technologies. The system bridges the gap between citizens and authorities by enabling real-time monitoring and automated complaint management.

In GovTrack, any registered citizen and any resident affected by a civic issue can raise an issue or file the complaint.

GovTrack addresses delays and lack of transparency in traditional complaint systems. It digitizes the process, reduces manual dependency and ensures faster routing and tracking of civic issues.

GovTrack uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) to analyze complaint descriptions and classify the issue type. Based on predefined mappings between issue categories and departments, the system automatically forwards the complaint to the appropriate authority without manual intervention, ensuring faster and more accurate processing.

Unlike conventional civic complaint platforms, GovTrack integrates AI-based image validation, NLP-driven auto-routing, interactive geospatial mapping and a public transparency dashboard.

The system uses AI-based classification where grievances are automatically sent to the concerned department based on the complaint type.
